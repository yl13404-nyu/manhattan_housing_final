Manhattan Housing Growth, Rent Growth, and Rent Burden

New York housing debates often assume that adding housing slows rent increases, and that neighborhoods already burdened by rent face the steepest increases. This dashboard lets you check both ideas across ten Manhattan areas for any pair of years from 2006 to 2025. Choose a base year and an end year and the three views update together.
